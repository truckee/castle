How to nominate a neighbor

Nominations should include the first and last name, street address and email address of the person making the nomination. The email address is requested so we can provide instructions on how to submit a photo of the nominee. The photo will be included on the Meet Your Neighbor page. The nomination should also include the first and last name plus their email address of the nominee along with a sentence or two of the reason for nomination. The nominee's email address allows us to confirm their agreement to appearing on the website.

When you're ready, log in to your TownSq account. In the left hand column, under Communication, click on Forum. Find the forum subject Nominate and enter your nomination. An addition to the existing Meet Your Neighbor website feature will be created by the webmaster. A member of the HOA's board will review the web page addition before it may be posted. You will get an email when we're done.
